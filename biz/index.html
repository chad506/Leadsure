What Changed Since v3

July 29 → August 11, 2026 · two weeks of movement
  • All 20 of the v3 top twenty are still on the market — nothing sold outright in the two weeks since July 29.
  • One went under contract: the University Place booth-rental salon now shows Pending.
  • One material price cut: the Sammamish Pure Barre studio went from $199,000 to $99,000, a 50% reduction.
  • The Pure Barre detail page also corrects the v3 write-up — the studio was established in 2025 and grosses $81,117, not the mature franchise v3 implied.
  • No other listing in the top twenty changed its asking price or disclosed cash flow.

Raising the ceiling to $350K added 145 listings and only three that belong on the board.

Of the 145 active Washington listings between $200,001 and $350,000, 70 are food service — restaurants, teriyaki counters, delis, cafes, bakeries and bars — and only 63 disclose cash flow at all. Six are delivery routes. The band is thin on exactly what this ranking looks for: businesses someone else runs. Three cleared the bar (the absentee dog daycare, the express tunnel car wash, and the membership massage franchise) and they enter at ranks 1, 5 and 7. The read: in Washington, semi-absentee structure under $350K is scarce enough that the extra $150,000 of budget buys mostly restaurants.

Methodology & Sources

Version 4, August 11, 2026. Screen: all 419 active Washington State business-for-sale listings at or under $350,000 on BizBuySell, every category, re-examined listing-by-listing for ownership models: absentee and semi-absentee claims, booth-rent and station-rental structures, membership and recurring revenue, contractor and manager-run staffing, and multi-unit oversight models. The price ceiling was raised from the $200,000 used in v3; the 145 listings in the new $200,001–$350,000 band were screened on the same basis as the 274 below $200,000. The scoring criteria are unchanged from v3 (cash flow 1–5, owner involvement 1–5 ×2, barrier to entry 1–5, repeat customers 1–5, low/no inventory 1–5; max 30), and per curator direction all Mission Foods tortilla routes remain excluded. Listings with undisclosed cash flow were ranked only where the operating structure is verifiably semi-absentee (fully absentee claims, staffed studios, booth rent, membership franchises, manager-run multi-unit) and every one of them is flagged for NDA diligence. *Days listed remain estimates derived from BizBuySell ad-ID sequencing calibrated against 60+ dated Internet Archive snapshots (±4–6 weeks) — treat them as approximate. The Owner-Active Value Board and the Flagged table carry over unchanged from the v3 screen of July 29, 2026 and were not re-verified in this run. Category sold benchmarks: BizBuySell valuation-benchmark reports (service, cleaning, dry cleaners, routes, printing, hair salons & barber shops, spas, medical practices, pet care, car washes, retail, building & construction). All listing figures are seller-reported and unverified. Nothing on this page is investment, legal, or tax advice — verify everything with tax returns, bank statements, and professional diligence before any offer.
