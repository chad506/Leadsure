Methodology & Honest Caveats

Fair values use a zero-drift lognormal diffusion on the challenger/leader market-cap ratio: P(flip) = Φ(ln(capchal/caplead) / σ√T), T in trading days (3 / 24 / 109), base σrel = 2%/day with a 1.5–2.5% sensitivity band. That model assumes no persistent relative drift — precisely the assumption the market rejects (Mispricing #2), so treat "edge" as model-vs-market disagreement, not guaranteed profit.
