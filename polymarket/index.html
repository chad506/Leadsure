Methodology & Honest Caveats

Fair values use a zero-drift lognormal diffusion on the challenger/leader market-cap ratio: P(flip) = Φ(ln(capchal/caplead) / σ√T), T in trading days (2 / 23 / 108), base σrel = 2%/day with a 1.5–2.5% sensitivity band. That model assumes no persistent relative drift — precisely the assumption the market rejects (Mispricing #2), so treat "edge" as model-vs-market disagreement, not guaranteed profit. Note the regime change baked into this snapshot: Apple passed NVIDIA intraday Jul 27, so "challenger" and "leader" swapped seats mid-series; the model (page and JS both) keys off whichever company is largest on the tick, and the leader’s own leg is computed as 100 − ΣP(challengers) − small legs. Empirical odds-move stats use ~32 daily CLOB closes (13 for the August book) and include weekend sessions and the flip session. Mid prices are not executable: July Apple top-of-book at this snapshot was 556 sh × 91.7¢ bid / 250 × 92.7¢ ask.
