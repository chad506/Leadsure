Methodology & Honest Caveats

Fair values use a zero-drift lognormal diffusion on the challenger/leader market-cap ratio: P(flip) = Φ(ln(capchal/caplead) / σ√T), T in trading days (5 / 26 / 111), base σrel = 2%/day with a 1.5–2.5% sensitivity band. That model assumes no persistent relative drift — precisely the assumption the market rejects (Mispricing #2), so treat "edge" as model-vs-market disagreement, not guaranteed profit. Note the regime change baked into this snapshot: Apple passed NVIDIA intraday Jul 27, so "challenger" and "leader" swapped seats mid-series; fair values for NVIDIA are computed as 100 − ΣP(challengers) − small legs. Empirical odds-move stats use ~30 daily CLOB closes (11 for the August book) and include weekend sessions and the flip session. Mid prices are not executable: July top-of-book at this snapshot was 10 sh × 53.9¢ bid / 303 × 54.9¢ ask. Odds snapshot Jul 27, 2026 15:00 UTC; caps Jul 27 intraday (Finnhub ~14:55 UTC). Nothing here is financial advice; prediction markets can resolve against you on oracle judgment alone.
